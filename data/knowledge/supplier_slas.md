# Supplier SLAs

## Produce delivery window

Produce arrives 06:00–09:00. Reject if pulp temperature is above 41°F. Photo the rejection and email the supplier the same morning.

## Dairy and meat

Dairy by 10:00. Meat sealed, dated, and at or below 41°F. Short-ships must be noted on the PO before the driver leaves.

## Dry goods and packaging

Two business days lead time for non-critical SKUs. Critical packaging (pizza boxes, cups) has a same-day emergency SKU list with the backup supplier.

## Invoice disputes

Flag price variance over 3% within 48 hours. Do not pay a duplicate invoice; mark it in the AP queue.
