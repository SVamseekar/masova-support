# Labor Compliance

## Breaks and maximum hours

Adults: unpaid 30-minute meal after 6 hours, 15-minute paid rest per 4 hours. Minors: no more than 8 hours/day on school days. Managers must not edit punches without a written reason.

## Overtime

Hours over 40 in the work week are overtime. Split shifts that span more than 10 hours need a premium. Do not schedule back-to-back close/open without 10 hours rest.

## Youth restrictions

No fryer, slicer, or delivery driving for staff under 18. School-night end time 9pm.

## Timekeeping

Clock in at the store, not remotely. Missed punches go to the manager queue the same day.
