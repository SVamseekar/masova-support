# Food Safety HACCP

## Cooler and Refrigeration Temperatures

Cold-holding of TCS foods must stay at or below 41°F (5°C). Check cooler temperature range at open, mid-shift, and close. If a unit reads above 41°F, move product to a working cooler, discard food held above 41°F for more than 4 hours, and log the corrective action.

## Hot Holding and Cooking

Hot-hold at 135°F (57°C) or above. Poultry cook to 165°F (74°C). Log probe thermometer calibration daily.

## Handwashing and Cross-Contamination

Wash hands after handling raw protein, before plating, and after restroom use. Use separate boards for raw and ready-to-eat. Color-code knives.

## Allergen Control

Flag dairy, gluten, peanut, and shellfish on tickets. Change gloves after allergen tickets. Never use the same fryer oil for allergen and non-allergen items when a dedicated fryer exists.
